# LearnHarness First Week Plan

Copy this file, fill the blanks, run the week. One topic, five short sessions, a graph with real depth at the end.

The rules that make it work:

1. Answer the probe honestly. The plan is only as good as the edge it starts from.
2. Use the thinking field. Reasoning out loud is signal the system uses.
3. One topic all week. Depth compounds, breadth evaporates.

## Day 1: Teach, first probe

Open a Teach session. Paste your own version of:

```
I want to understand [TOPIC]. [One sentence on why.]
My honest starting point: [your ceiling from above].
```

- Answer every probe question without performing. "I don't know" is a good answer.
- Review the plan it draws. Revise it in plain language if it starts too early or too late.
- Work the first step or two. Stop while it is still comfortable.

**After the session, note the step the plan ended on:** ____________________

## Day 2: Teach, continue the plan

Reopen Teach on the same topic. The session can read your graph, so it knows what landed yesterday.

- Work the next steps. Predict before every explanation, even when you are sure.
- Flag anything fuzzy immediately. Fuzzy now is a gap later.

## Day 3: Teach, continue

Same as Day 2. If a step collapses into "wait, I never understood the step before it," say exactly that. Replanning from your real edge is the feature, not a detour.

## Day 4: Teach, finish or import

Either finish the plan, or stop teaching and feed the graph instead:

- Graph view → **Extract files… → Files** or **Folder**
- Add your existing notes on the topic (Markdown, plain text, or text-based PDFs, 25 MiB max per file, three at a time)

## Day 5: Practice

Open a Practice session. Start from your graph material and let it pick at the thin coverage.

```
Drill me on this week's material. Prioritize whatever I have the
weakest coverage on.
```

- Work problems until one genuinely stumps you. That one is next week's Day 1.
