## Structure

- **Hook (0 to 3s):** what is said, what is on screen.
- **Promise (3 to 6s):** the number, how it is staged.
- **Steps:** how each step is labeled (kicker, number, progress bar), how long each one runs.
- **Save or follow beat:** if any.
- **CTA (last 4 to 6s):** the mechanic and how it is staged.

## Pacing

- **Average beat:** new visual every N seconds.
- **Shortest beat:** N seconds.
- **Longest hold:** N seconds, and what holds.

## Typography

- **Headlines:** family, size, words per title, how the key word is emphasized.
- **Body and labels:** family, size, case.
- **Code or terminal text:** family, size.

- **Scene changes:** cut, push, slide, and the duration.
- **Loops:** cursors, waveforms, floating marks.
