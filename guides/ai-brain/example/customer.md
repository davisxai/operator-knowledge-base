# Customer: the builder who ships with AI tools

Which customer. The profile Davis writes for on Instagram. One stage gets picked per piece.

## 01 Who

A builder without a traditional engineering background who builds with Claude Code, MCP, n8n, Lovable, Bolt, and Cursor. Wins through orchestration, taste, and system design. Watches AI content to level up process, not to learn syntax.

## 02 Stage

Has shipped:
- nothing yet. The tinkerer. Plays with the tools, wants a process to follow, wants proof someone like them can win.
- one thing. The builder who shipped once. Got some traction, stuck on the next level, knows what works in theory.
- paid work. The operator. Already bills for AI work, wants to level up the stack and the systems.

Pick one per piece. Tinkerers get quick wins and playbooks. Shipped-once builders get system breakdowns and templates. Operators get operations, pricing, and scoping.

## 03 Wants

Serious process. How a real system is built, in the order it was built, with the files.

## 04 Responds to

A resource that maps a workflow end to end. Templates, checklists, the real config.

## 05 Never responds to

- Hype. "This changes everything."
- Guru cadence. "What most people don't realize."
- Effortless outcomes. "How I made $X in Y days."

## 06 Not for

People who watch AI content and never build. Course buyers looking for the next guru. Anyone looking for free consulting in the DMs.
