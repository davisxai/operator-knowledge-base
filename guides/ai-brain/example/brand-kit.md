# Brand kit: OperatorOS

Palette, type, logo, accent rules. Claude reads this before it designs a slide, a graphic, or a page. Every slide in the AI Brain carousel was built from this file. logo.png sits next to it.

## Colors

- background: #0a0a0a
- foreground: #fafafa
- primary / ring / destructive: #d2042d. Cherry red. The one accent color.
- card: #111111
- muted: #191919
- border / input: #232323
- Light surfaces for print and slides: white and warm paper. The same red carries over.

## Typography

- Display face: Helvetica Neue. Hierarchy through weight, not through more fonts.
- hero: 48 / semibold
- h1: text-3xl / semibold
- h2: text-2xl / semibold
- h3: text-lg / semibold
- body: 16 / 24
- label: text-xs / semibold, uppercase, tracked
- Serif claim headlines on carousel slides, one accent phrase in red.
- Monospace: for file names, code, card bodies, and terminal content only.

## Logo

- File: logo.png. The knot mark.
- Placement: center top. On carousel slides it sits in the header strip between the handle and the domain.
- Never recolored. Never stretched.

## Accent rule

- One accent word or phrase per frame, in #d2042d. Never a whole paragraph in red.
- The accent also carries step numbers, stat numbers, bracketed placeholders inside prompt cards, and the CTA keyword.
- On dark slides the secondary line is white at reduced weight. Red paragraphs on black are the lowest-contrast text in the deck, so they do not exist.

## Backgrounds

- Dark (#0a0a0a) and light (warm paper) alternate slide by slide. The cover is light.
- Faint symmetric line pattern as a watermark on both.

## Screenshots and images

- Real captures only. No stock images, no fake mockups.
- Slight rotation and a drop shadow when a capture is the visual.
- Client names, dollar amounts, and email addresses never appear in a capture.

## UI

- Dark native. shadcn components. Small corner radius.
- Sidebar workspace, top tab bar, command menu on Cmd K, quick create on C.
