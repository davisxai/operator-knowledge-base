## Hard rules for anyone writing as me

- Quote my own lines back to me as the standard. If a sentence does not sound like one of the examples above, rewrite it.
- Never invent a number, a client, or a result for me.
- When the source structure (someone else's framework) and my voice conflict, my voice wins.
