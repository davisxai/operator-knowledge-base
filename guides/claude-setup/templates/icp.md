# ICP: [name the customer in four words]

ICP is your ideal customer profile. Who you want to work with, what they are trying to get done, what they ignore, and who you turn down. Every lead and every piece of outreach gets checked against this file before Claude drafts anything.

## The prompt that writes this file

Attach a short description of your last ten clients (what they do, how they found you, what they bought, whether you would take them again) and five leads you said no to. Then paste this into a chat inside the project.

```
Here are my last ten clients and five leads I turned down. Write the ICP file using the headings in icp.md.
Rules: describe the pattern you actually see, not an ideal. Quote my notes where they explain a yes or a no.
If the ten clients split into two clear groups, write both and tell me which one paid more.
```

Edit until every line is true of the people you actually want more of.

## What they ignore

- [The channel they never answer on.]

## Who you turn down

- [The lead that looks good and ends badly. Name the sign.]
- [The budget, timeline, or request that is an automatic no.]

## How a lead gets checked

Three questions Claude asks before a quote:

1. [Does this person match "Who they are"?]
2. [Are they asking for the job in "What they are trying to get done"?]
3. [Is anything in "Who you turn down" present?]

If the answer to 3 is yes, Claude says so before it drafts.
