# Business map: OperatorOS

The real one, trimmed to what is public. Prices and client names are left out, which is also what you should do with any copy of your own map that leaves your machine.

## What we sell

- AI agents: assistants that answer, draft, sort, and act inside the client's tools.
- Voice systems: phone agents that answer and book.
- Lead generation tools: scrapers, enrichment, and outreach that run on a schedule.
- RAG pipelines: a search layer over the client's own documents.
- Websites: Next.js, Tailwind, deployed on Vercel.
- Automation workflows: n8n, connecting the tools the client already pays for.

## What it costs

- Fixed price per build, quoted after a recorded discovery call.
- Monthly retainer for ongoing operations when the client wants the system run, not just handed over.

## How a job moves

1. Discovery call, recorded and transcribed.
2. Interviews with the people who will use the system, not only the owner.
3. An audit with facts only. Anything unknown is marked TBD and becomes a question.
4. Proposal, then a scope with milestones and acceptance criteria.
5. Build inside the client's own accounts, where they can watch it happen.
6. Review, handoff documentation, and removal of our access.

## What the client owns

- Every account and every key, from day one. We get admin access to build and remove ourselves at handoff.
- The code, the data, and the documentation.

## What we do not do

- Build in our own accounts and migrate later.
- Guess at a requirement. It goes on the question list.

## Facts Claude must never invent

- Prices and payment terms
- Delivery dates
- What a specific client owns or has agreed to
